Codes

I have collected example codes on my GitHub page that solve heterogeneous-agent models. These codes, written in Julia, solve an Aiyagari model in steady state and with transitional dynamics, as well as a one-asset HANK model. I hope they are useful for people who want to get familiar with these numerical techniques.
